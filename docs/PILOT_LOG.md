# Stakeholder pilot log

Purpose: record what people who are not us said about the product, without
collecting anything about the people.

**Status: no outreach has taken place.** No school, resident association or
other group has been contacted, because no recipient has been approved and
messaging people on someone's behalf is not something to improvise. The log
below is empty and will stay empty until that approval exists.

## Rules for this log

- No names, no contact details, no institution-level identification of an
  individual. A row says what kind of group, what they were shown, and what they
  said.
- Feedback is recorded as written or as a close paraphrase, including the
  unflattering parts. A pilot log that contains only praise is a marketing page.
- Nothing recorded here counts as a research result. It is not a user study,
  which needs ethics approval and lives in `docs/USER_STUDY.md`.
- Anyone may ask for their feedback to be removed; delete the row and note the
  deletion with its date.

## What a pilot session covers

1. Open the live site on the participant's own phone, on whatever connection
   they have. Observed load time and failures matter as much as opinions.
2. Ask them to find tomorrow's forecast for where they live, without help.
   Record what they tried first.
3. Ask what the number means and whether it was measured or modelled. This is
   where the measured-versus-modelled labelling either works or does not.
4. Ask what they would do differently tomorrow, if anything.
5. Record what they expected the app to do and did not find.

## Before a session can be held

1. Approved list of groups to approach, and approved wording for the first
   message. Outreach is an action taken in the project's name.
2. A decision on what to do if a participant asks for health advice: the answer
   is to point at their clinician, and the person running the session should
   have that sentence ready rather than improvising it.
3. Confirmation that the live site is in a state worth showing. It currently
   presents an uncorrected model forecast with its limitations stated, which is
   honest but is not a validated product; say so in the session.
