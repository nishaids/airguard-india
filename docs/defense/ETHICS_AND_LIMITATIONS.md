# Ethics and limitations

Environmental guidance must not imply diagnosis, medical treatment, zero exposure risk or an official CPCB forecast. Advisory cohorts represent exposure sensitivity contexts; they are not validated disease probabilities. Users with limited control over work or transport need options, not blame.

Under-warning risks false reassurance; excessive alerts cause fatigue and unequal opportunity costs. Report both and disclose severe misses. Uneven monitoring can favour well-observed cities; a modelled fallback improves availability but does not establish equal accuracy.

Intervals must show absence of validation and conditional failures. Explanations of feature importance describe model behaviour, not causal pollution sources. A counterfactual slider recalculates assumed concentrations; it cannot estimate the effect of a real policy intervention.

Publish provenance, QC exclusions, failed comparisons and remaining blockers. Preserve prior artifacts when correcting metadata. Product responsibility includes accessible error states, responsible location retention, commercial licensing and user testing before relying on the system at scale.
