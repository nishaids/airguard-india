# Why use AirGuard when AQI apps exist?

AirGuard’s defensible purpose is to connect tomorrow’s source-labelled forecast with Indian index conventions and practical exposure planning, while making missing measurements and validation limits visible. Existing portals, APIs and apps remain useful and may already provide forecasts or advice.

Scientifically, the project supplies an auditable station/day-ahead evaluation rather than assuming a physics forecast needs correction. For users, it combines provenance, temporal variation and advisory context in one flow. Engineering work makes provider failure and fallback explicit. Public benefit would depend on accurate warnings, usability and feasible action; none is established by a demo alone.

The case is strongest as a transparent research/prototype integration. It weakens if correction fails to beat raw CAMS, extreme events are missed, or users misunderstand the grid’s precision. Those are testable questions, not reasons to hide negative results.
