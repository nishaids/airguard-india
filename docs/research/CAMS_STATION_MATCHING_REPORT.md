## How a station is matched

- **Space**: nearest grid centre (station latitude and longitude each rounded to the 0.4-degree lattice); the cell must exist exactly once in the file. Grid: CAMS operational 0.4-degree grid.
- **Time**: native 3-hourly leads interpolated to hourly within the acquired leads; no extrapolation. Forecasts are 00 UTC runs issued the day before the target; observations obey the 20:00 IST cutoff.
- **Coast**: none: a coastal station can fall in a cell that is mostly sea; not yet audited.
