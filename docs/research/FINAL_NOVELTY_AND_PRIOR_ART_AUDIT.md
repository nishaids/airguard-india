Generated 2026-10-20T07:39:55Z by `scripts/build_novelty_audit.py`. Machine-readable: `results/research/literature_matrix.json`. This audit supersedes the bounded `NOVELTY_AUDIT.md` and builds on `LITERATURE_FULLTEXT_REVIEW.md`; nothing there is discarded.

## Verdict

AirGuard's individual methods are all established: station-level CAMS correction, gradient boosting and ridge models, rolling-origin and spatial validation, conformal intervals, cost-loss warnings and operational forecast ledgers each have prior art below, and India already runs operational early-warning systems. **No first-ever or state-of-the-art claim is supported.**

What was not found among the reviewed studies is the combination: archived operational CAMS forecasts corrected at CPCB-network monitors across about 100 Indian cities, under a code-enforced issue-time contract, judged by temporal and unseen-station transfer, with the open Severe band treated as censored and with coverage and under-warning reported by pollution category. That combination is a defensible *research design*. It becomes a *contribution* only when the primary CAMS experiment has run; today it has not (archive 9 of 36 files, no eligible fold).
